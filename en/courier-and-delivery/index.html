Industries

IT and software for courier firms

Customers book and follow the delivery themselves.

Courier firms and local delivery services get orders by phone and email, and customers ask where the delivery is. A booking page, a driver app and tracking remove many of the calls.

Common challenges

  • Orders by phone
  • Customers call about delivery status
  • Missing proof of delivery in disputes

What can be built

  • Online booking with an instant price
  • A driver app with routes and proof of delivery
  • A tracking link for the recipient
  • Invoicing per customer and period

Frequently asked questions

Can the price be calculated at booking?

Yes, from distance, weight or zones according to your rules.

Is the recipient notified?

Yes, by text or email when the delivery is on its way and when delivered.
