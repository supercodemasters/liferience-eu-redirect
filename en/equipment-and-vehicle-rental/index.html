Industries

IT and software for rental companies

The right unit free, booked and paid.

Renters of trailers, cars, machines, tools and equipment need availability per unit, bookings, contracts and handling of damage and deposits.

Common challenges

  • Availability tracked in a binder or spreadsheet
  • Contracts and checklists on paper
  • Damage and deposits followed up by hand

What can be built

  • Online booking per unit with availability
  • Digital contracts and handover checklists with photos
  • Deposit and payment at booking
  • Service and damage history per unit

Frequently asked questions

Can customers book and pay online?

Yes, with a deposit if you want.

Can we see when a machine needs service?

Yes. Service can be planned by time or number of rentals.
