Industries

IT and software for haulage companies

Jobs, vehicles and invoice records gathered together.

Haulage firms handle orders, runs, drivers, vehicles and consignment notes. When jobs arrive by phone and email and notes are on paper, invoicing and follow-up take a long time.

Common challenges

  • Jobs by phone and email
  • Consignment notes and receipts on paper
  • Invoicing lags behind deliveries

What can be built

  • Order entry and planning per vehicle
  • A driver app with jobs, proof of delivery and photos
  • Invoice records straight after delivery
  • A dashboard for runs, revenue and empty running

Frequently asked questions

Can the driver confirm delivery on mobile?

Yes. The recipient signs on the phone and the photo is saved with the job.

Does it work with our finance system?

Invoice records go to the finance system through a connection.
