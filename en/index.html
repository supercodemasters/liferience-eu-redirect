Frequently asked questions

Do the apps work without coverage?

Yes. The driver app can work offline and sync when the connection returns.

Do we have to change our systems?

Rarely. What is missing is built and connected to what you already have.

What does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After the first call you get a written proposal with a price.

Does it suit small hauliers?

Yes. Even a haulier with a few trucks can save a lot of time on consignment notes and invoicing.

Shall we have a short call?

Book a short video or phone call. The first call is free of charge.
