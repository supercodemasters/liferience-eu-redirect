Industries

IT and software for removal companies

From quote request to booked move without long calls.

Removal firms spend a lot of time on quotes: survey, volume, date and extras. A good form and a booking calendar let the customer describe the move and book once the quote is approved.

Common challenges

  • Quotes take a long time to produce
  • The calendar is kept in someone’s head or on paper
  • RUT deduction records are done by hand

What can be built

  • A quote form with volume, floor and extras
  • A quote the customer approves with a link
  • A booking calendar per team and van
  • Invoice records with RUT according to your routines

Frequently asked questions

Can the customer send photos of their belongings?

Yes. Photos and lists are uploaded in the form.

Does it handle the RUT deduction?

The basis can be prepared in the invoice according to your routines and your accounting firm.
